## Steps

1. Download Plex from the App Store: [Plex](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plex-find-movies-and-tv-shows/id383457673)
2. Open the app and sign up. Using a Google account is typically the easiest.
3. Send me a text with the email used during signup and I will give you access to the Server. Unfortunately this is the only way to do this otherwise I'd just paste a sign up URL here.
4. You should then recieve two emails:
    1. One email stating you have been invited to share some media in Plex. Click the "I accept the invitation" link at the bottom and sign in with your Plex account (same email you used for signup)
    2. Second email stating DrewKapnick5 (my Plex account) wants to be friends. Accept that too. I promise it is necessary or maybe I am just desparate for friends.
5. You should now have access to my Plex libraries. Sometimes it can take a few minutes to sync up. You may have to kill the app and restart it. If you are having issues, text me, we are friends now after all.
6. Open the Plex app, tap Libraries at the bottom, tap the Movies dropdown at the top, and tap the Heart Icon for each library you are interested in. This step isn't necessary but it pins the libraries on the Home tab and makes them easier to access.
7. You can now change libraries with the Dropdown at the top of the screen, and navigate via Recommended, Browse, or Categories tabs or search for a movie directly. You're smart, I know you can figure it out.
