# Plex Setup

<p class="guide-intro">
Plex is the primary way to access the Movies and TV Shows I have on my server. This will be a short tutorial on how to gain access and set it up on an iPhone, however, Plex is available on almost any device and the setup is very similar. Once you properly setup access to my server, all you have to do is sign in to your Plex account on any additional devices.
</p>
