Requesting Content

Previous

I try to keep a lot of content already on my servers but obviously I don't have everything and everyone's tastes are different. If you want to request something new and have it show up automatically, you have to request it with the Pocket app.

Plex = Watch stuff Pocket = Request new stuff

Tap to play — swipe/scrub like a normal video

Setup Steps

  1. Download Pocket from the App Store: Pocket
  2. Open the app, click Get Started and enter the URL seerr.drewpedia.org (make sure you type both "r"s in "seerr")
  3. Tap the blue check, and choose Plex Login.
  4. Tap Continue if it prompts to use "app.plex.tv: to Sign In, and sign in with your Plex account.
  5. If you see a Discover tab with a bunch of Movies and Shows displayed then you are setup.

Request Steps

As I said, I generally try to request most popular content already myself but if I am missing something or there is a new show coming out you'd like to request then its pretty self explanatory but I'll lay out the steps anyway. Also, I have a good amount of storage on my server so I genuinely want you to feel free to request content that you want to watch, however its not unlimited so just keep that in mind. If we ever get to that point where space becomes an issue I may have to delete some content that noone really watches but I am not worried about that at the moment.

  1. Search for the movie or show you'd like with the search bar or navigating the categories
  2. For movies, simply tap Request and Request again in the popup
  3. For shows, tap Request and select the toggle per Season or the top toggle for all Seasons.
  4. If it is an upcoming movie or weekly released show, Requesting ahead of time is perfectly fine and will download the content when it becomes available.
  5. Requested content typically takes between 5-30 minutes to download and become available. If it takes longer than that don't panic, its probably just lagging begind a bit. If it takes way longer than that, report an issue or send me a text.

Reporting an Issue

The entire process is fully automated and generally does a good job, but as always with automation, sometimes mistakes happen. So if the episode audio is in another language or the video appears glitchy etc. then you can use the Pocket app to report an issue. I would prefer this over you just texting me something is wrong, but if it is a really big issue or I haven't fixed your issue then you can of course send me a text.

  1. Open Pocket app and search for movie/show with the issue.
  2. Tap the Three dots icon in the upper right.
  3. If its a show, select the appropriate season/episode.
  4. Select the Type and enter a small description of what the issue is.
  5. Click Report
  6. You can view yours, or other posted issues in the Issues tab.

Other Things to Note:

  1. This is just my personal sever running off my apartment internet. It should be able to support a good amount of users but in theory, as I give more people access, it could start to slow down. This really shouldn't be an issue but I just wanted to call it out in case we all start noticing some slowdowns. If this truly starts becoming an issue, I'll probably already be working on it. This also means if my apartment internet is down for whatever reason, then obviously no Plex for anyone. I don't see this being an issue often but sorry if it ever happens.
  2. Shows with a weekly release date can sometimes take a bit to download after its actual release. Sometimes they download very quickly. It's kind of just up to when it becomes available. Generally if an episode is supposed to drop on Wednesday night, it will be available by Thursday morning.
  3. At the time of writing this, some new shows occaisionally have issues with weekly releases not downloading properly. I am working on fixing this but for now if you notice it hasn't shown up a day or two after its release date, either report an issue or send me a text.
  4. Behind the scenes, I am trying a new method of downloading TV Shows. I won't bore you with the techno-babble but it's possible you could see a couple shows not working properly now and then. If that's the case, you guessed it, report an issue or send me a text.
  5. The Pocket app can sometimes be a bit slow at marking something as "Available" after requesting it. Generally it will probably be available to watch in Plex before it actually displays as available in Pocket.
  6. If you have an upcoming flight and want to download some content from my server, send me a text and we will figure something out. Plex used to let you do this for free but now they require you to have a Plex Pass (classic corporate greed).
